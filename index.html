The usual questions.

Is this only for investment firms?

No. Deal sourcing is where it's proven, but the same architecture runs media lists, monitoring, briefs, triage, and reporting.

How is this different from Harmonic, Specter, or Affinity?

Those index a fixed universe. RennerFlow is built for your firm: your sources, your taxonomy, your CRM. Most clients run both.

Which tools do you write into?

Anything with an API: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Affinity, Airtable, Sheets, Notion, Slack, custom webhooks.

How long until it is live?

Three to five weeks from scoping call to first live run. Firm timeline after the call.

What about data privacy?

Dedicated infrastructure per client, Gmail OAuth for email. You can also host it in your own cloud.

Who is already using this?
