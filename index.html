FAQ

The usual questions.

Most intro calls start with these. Open them before we speak and the call goes twice as far.

How is this different from Harmonic, Specter, or Affinity?
Those platforms index a fixed universe of companies and give every customer the same interface. RennerFlow is a pipeline built for your firm: your sources, your taxonomy, your CRM schema. Most clients run it alongside one of those platforms — use them for broad discovery, use RennerFlow for the sources they don't index and for structured CRM input.
Which CRMs do you support?
Anything with an API or form endpoint. Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce, Affinity, Airtable, Pipedrive, custom webhooks — all straightforward. Unusual in-house systems get handled in scoping.
How long until it's live?
Usually three to five weeks from scoping call to first live run. Longer if your source list is unusually custom or the CRM schema needs heavy mapping. You'll have a firm timeline after the scoping call.
What about data privacy?
Pipelines run on infrastructure dedicated to your fund — not a shared system. Email processing uses Gmail OAuth; no IMAP credentials stored. You can also opt to host the whole pipeline inside your own cloud account if your firm requires it.
Who's already using this?
An ag/foodtech VC has been running both source-monitoring and team-forward channels in production since April 2026. Happy to make an intro on request.
